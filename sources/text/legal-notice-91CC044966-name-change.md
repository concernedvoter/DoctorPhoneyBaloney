Published legal notice, Circuit Court of Boone County, case no. 91CC044966: name of Abdulrahman Mohamed changed to Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed by order of Dec 9, 1991. Newspaper not identified in the clipping.

Clipping image (cropped; newspaper name, page and date line not visible; yellow highlights were added by the researcher, not the paper). Provenance of the clipping to be recorded.

Text, as printed:
IN THE CIRCUIT COURT OF BOONE COUNTY, MISSOURI. IN RE: Abdulrahman Mohamed by Next Friend Mohamed El-Sayed. Case No. 91CC044966. TO WHOM IT MAY CONCERN: NOTICE is hereby given that on December 9, 1991 by order of the Circuit Court of Boone County, the name of Abdulrahman Mohamed was changed to that of Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed. Cheryl Whitmarsh, Circuit Clerk. By: Margaret Thomas, Deputy Clerk. Insertion Dates: December 20, 27, 1991 and January 3, 1992.

What it shows:
- The notice ran three times: Dec 20 and 27, 1991 and Jan 3, 1992 (answers the search window; it falls inside Nov 14, 1991 to Jan 31, 1992).
- 'By Next Friend Mohamed El-Sayed' is the form used when a minor is represented by another person, so the petitioner was a minor in 1991. The notice does not state the next friend's relationship to the minor or the child's age.
- Case number and date match the CaseNet docket 1391CC044966 (docket spells the new name 'Abdlrahman'; the notice spells it 'Abdulrahman').
What it does not show: the old surname on a birth record, the birthplace, the parents' names, or which newspaper ran it.
