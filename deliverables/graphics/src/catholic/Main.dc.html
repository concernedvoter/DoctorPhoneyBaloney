HOW THE BISHOPS ASK CATHOLICS TO WEIGH IT
Abortion and euthanasia are intrinsic evils and carry special weight, so the horizontal axis counts more. Immigration, health care, war, guns and creation are matters of prudential judgment: Catholics may disagree on means, not on principles. When every candidate falls short, Faithful Citizenship (paragraphs 34 to 37) says a voter may choose the one judged less likely to advance grave harms, and should weigh character and integrity too.
