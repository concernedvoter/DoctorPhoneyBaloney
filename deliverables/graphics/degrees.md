# Degrees of separation: El-Sayed and the Muslim Brotherhood

Seven paths traced link by link from the public record. A step is a contact or relationship, not membership or agreement. Line style encodes rating: solid Documented, dashed Reported, dotted Disputed. Shortest path: 1 step (his own 2012 letter and 2021 post). Evidence that he or his family are members: none found.

**Weakest link.** National CAIR to the Palestine Committee. A 1994 committee agenda lists CAIR on a line marked "Coordination." In 2009 Judge Solis found a prima facie case of CAIR's involvement and sealed the co-conspirator list. CAIR was never charged and denies any tie to Hamas. Its co-founder Omar Ahmad is documented as a committee member.

**Weakest link (paths 2, 4, 6 and 7).** Paths 2 and 4 pass through Mohamed Soltan: his 2015 Egyptian conviction is Documented, but "funding the Brotherhood" is Disputed, "in absentia" conflicts with the record, and his tie to his father is family. Path 6 rests on a characterization (IPT's "orbit"), Disputed. Path 7 rests on two directory entries, Reported, with the exhibit not read.

**What the record does not show.** No evidence that El-Sayed or his family belongs to the Brotherhood. Paths through relatives, donors or group histories do not describe his own acts. Nobody is accused of wrongdoing by appearing here.
