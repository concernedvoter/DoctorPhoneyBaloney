# Issue by issue against Church teaching

Ratings: Aligned, Partly aligned, In tension, No clear position. GRAVE = intrinsic evil or grave matter in the bishops' framework; PRUDENTIAL = judgment on means; CHARACTER = truthfulness and dignity. Ratings are this guide's judgment from the record, not a Church verdict. The Church does not endorse candidates.

| Issue | Weight | Rogers | Rogers basis | El-Sayed | El-Sayed basis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Abortion | GRAVE | Partly aligned | Pro-life House record (100 percent National Right to Life). Since 2024 pledges not to change Michigan's abortion amendment; backs rape and incest exceptions. | In tension | Pledges to fight to protect abortion rights (campaign site, Oct. 2026). |
| IVF and embryos | GRAVE | In tension | Calls IVF life-affirming and wants insurers required to cover it (2024 op-ed). | In tension | Supports IVF access (campaign site). |
| Euthanasia | GRAVE | No clear position | No statement found. | No clear position | No statement found. |
| Marriage and gender | GRAVE | Aligned | Opposed redefining marriage; opposes males in girls' sports. | In tension | Backed trans youth access to sports teams of choice (2018); says patients and parents decide gender care for minors. |
| Religious liberty | GRAVE | Aligned | Strong House record; little 2026 detail. | No clear position | Defends Muslim civil rights; no position found on conscience protections. |
| Death penalty | GRAVE | No clear position | No statement found. The Catechism calls it inadmissible. | No clear position | No statement found. |
| Immigration and ICE | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Backs enforcing the law and removing criminals, which Scripture and Catechism 2241 support; the bishops object to indiscriminate deportation and detention conditions. | Partly aligned | Backs a path to citizenship; would abolish ICE, which the Church does not ask. |
| Health care and the poor | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Drug price and transparency plan; no position on Medicaid cuts. | Partly aligned | Medicare for All and restored Medicaid; said in 2018 that comprehensive care includes abortion access (Reported). |
| Iran war | PRUDENTIAL | In tension | Backed the war; now says it must end quickly. Pope Leo: there is no just war there. | Aligned | Opposed the war and favors diplomacy. |
| Israel and Gaza | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Staunch support for Israel's self-defense; no Gaza humanitarian statement found. | Partly aligned | Sharp critic of Israel's government; would condition military aid. |
| Guns | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Defends the right to bear arms, consistent with the right of legitimate defense (Catechism 2264 to 2265; see Luke 22:36); would not answer on universal background checks, which the bishops support. | In tension | Floated replacing the right to bear arms (2023, Reported), which conflicts with the right of self-defense in Scripture (Exodus 22:2, Luke 11:21, Luke 22:36) and Catechism 2264 to 2265. His assault weapons ban does match the bishops' policy positions. |
| Care for creation | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Opposes EV mandates; no climate position found. | Partly aligned | Backed Green New Deal goals in 2019; opposes an EV mandate. |
| Workers and labor | PRUDENTIAL | Partly aligned | Mixed House record; backed the 2023 UAW strike. | Aligned | Backs organized labor; endorsed by the UAW, AFL-CIO and Teamsters. |
| School choice | PRUDENTIAL | No clear position | No specific 2026 position found. | No clear position | Backs Education Department funding; no school choice position found. |
| Torture | GRAVE | In tension | As Intelligence chairman, defended the CIA interrogation program as legal and authorized (2014). | No clear position | No record found. |
| Truthfulness and dignity | CHARACTER | Partly aligned | Points to documented one-step contacts (2012 Morsi letter, Soltan post); claims that he was bought off or has Hamas donors went beyond the evidence shown. | In tension | Statements that conflict with the record: denied family Brotherhood ties though his half-sister wrote their mother was active (Reported); said his sister-in-law sued and got off the watch list, which the court record does not show; campaign says he respects the Second Amendment after he floated replacing it and made the video private. Listed Egyptian citizenship on a 2013 CV and claimed dual citizenship in a 2016 talk; his campaign now says he is not a dual citizen. Aides would not answer on his 2012 Morsi letter. At a Caro town hall he answered "Sure, yeah, absolutely" to disavowing the Brotherhood, then turned it into a heated challenge of the questioner's motives (video). |
