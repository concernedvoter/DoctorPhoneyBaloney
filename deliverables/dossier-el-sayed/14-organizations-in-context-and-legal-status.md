## Organizations in context and legal status

Of the groups below, the Egyptian, Jordanian and Lebanese Brotherhood branches carry federal terrorism designations, and the Islamic American Relief Agency was sanctioned. CAIR carries state designations in Texas and Florida that are being fought in federal court.
