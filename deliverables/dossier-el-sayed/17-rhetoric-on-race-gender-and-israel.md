## Rhetoric on race, gender and Israel

Critics cite these statements as evidence of hostility toward groups. The words are documented; whether they show bigotry is disputed, and fact-checkers rejected the broadest charges. The 2018 "Muslims hate you" retort is covered in Stage 6.

- **Temple Israel, March 2026.** After an attempted attack on the West Bloomfield synagogue, he condemned it and affirmed Jews' right to worship safely, then noted that the suspect had lost family in an Israeli airstrike in Lebanon: "Hurt people hurt people. Violence is a cycle" ([Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/03/abdul-el-sayed-temple-israel-attack-statement-risk)). He told supporters afterward that the statement was a risk. At the state party convention on August 29, 2026 he told Jewish Democrats he had made a mistake and apologized ([JTA via Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/jewish-telegraphic-agency/abdul-el-sayed-apologizes-for-temple-israel-comments-in-meeting-with-michigan-jewish-dems/article_3c663dd9-76d1-49ff-867d-d83947e5320a.html)). He had used the same phrase in 2023 about children in the county's juvenile system (see Stage 7).
- **Israel and Hamas, April 2026.** Asked on CNN's Inside Politics whether the Israeli government is as evil as Hamas, he said: "Hamas: Evil, Israeli government: Evil. We can say both" ([Fox News via WFMD](https://www.wfmd.com/2026/04/20/michigan-democratic-senate-candidate-claims-israel-just-as-evil-as-hamas/)). Confirm against the CNN broadcast. He also calls the Gaza war a genocide; see Adjacent controversies.
- **Israel's right to exist, 2026.** Asked three times by CNN's Kasie Hunt in July whether Israel has a right to exist, he said Israel exists, called the question a gotcha and said it was secondary to U.S. aid ([Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/cnn-anchor-asks-abdul-el-sayed-three-times-if-israel-has-a-right-to-exist/)). At a Jews for Abdul event in May he said he struggles with the question of Israel as a Jewish state ([Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/05/abdul-el-sayed-struggles-israel-right-to-exist-jewish-state/)).
- **Usha Vance, August 2026.** After Vice President Vance said he wished he could tell his grandfather about a candidate who backed Sharia, El-Sayed posted: "So do we think JD is taking Usha with him back in time to meet Papaw, or no" ([New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/214996/jd-vance-el-sayed-attacks-usha)). Republican critics read it as a jab at the Indian American second lady's ethnicity; the New Republic read it as aimed at Vance's own rhetoric. Characterizing it as racist is opinion.
- **Haley Stevens, June 2026.** In a Semafor interview he called his primary rival "a suit with a large AIPAC bank account" and said he hoped someone could teach her to string coherent sentences together ([Mediaite](https://www.mediaite.com/politics/please-stop-progressive-michigan-candidate-asks-supporters-to-stop-attacking-opponent-personally/)). On July 17 he asked supporters to stop mocking her, calling it unkind and unhelpful ([The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/campaign/5975272-el-sayed-urges-supporters-stop-stevens-attacks/)).
- **Women generally.** Bridge Michigan found that the examples in ads accusing him of disrespecting women do not show such a pattern. One ad cited a federal suit filed in April 2019 by eight employees against Mount Sinai and leaders of its Arnhold Institute ([Becker's](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/legal-regulatory-issues/mount-sinai-faces-lawsuit-over-age-sex-discrimination-in-its-global-health-institute/)). A 2022 complaint in the case alleges he said he did not want "anyone over 40" on a young team; he is not a defendant, the plaintiff does not accuse him of gender discrimination, and the case is ongoing ([Bridge Michigan](https://bridgemi.com/michigan-government/fact-check-ads-take-el-sayed-out-of-context-to-claim-he-disrespects-women/)).
- **Workplace records.** No lawsuit, EEOC charge, grievance or ethics complaint by a Detroit or Wayne County employee naming him was found in this review.

**Update, October 24, 2026.** In August 2026 he also posted to Trump aide Natalie Harp, "Natalie, I think he's had a little too much iPad for the week. He needs his rest." Republican Tudor Dixon wrote that he "picks on women who are not in his fight." Of Haley Stevens he later said, "the congresswoman is plenty smart, there's no doubt about that" (Vox). The Detroit Free Press, like Bridge Michigan, found the ads alleging disrespect of women did not support a pattern. No woman who worked for him has gone on record alleging sexism; his communications director, Roxie Richner, has defended him in her official role. Reported (Fox News, New York Post, Vox and the Free Press, through secondary research).
