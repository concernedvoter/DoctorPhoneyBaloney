## Bottom line

The record shows multiple, mostly public ties between El-Sayed and two Muslim American institutions, CAIR and ISNA. They run through speeches, donors, super PAC money and his father-in-law, a former CAIR-Michigan president.

It does not show him advocating that Sharia replace or override U.S. law. [PolitiFact](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2026/aug/10/tweets/abdul-el-sayed-sharia-muslim-cair-michigan-senate/) rated claims that he called for Sharia in America Pants on Fire, and [Bridge Michigan](https://www.bridgemi.com/michigan-government/fact-check-no-evidence-abdul-el-sayed-pledged-support-to-muslim-brotherhood/) and [Fox News Digital](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/unearthed-clip-exposes-clash-between-el-sayed-ex-gop-rival-muslims-hate-you) found no evidence of a personal Muslim Brotherhood tie. His platform, including abortion rights and LGBTQ rights, runs against a Sharia-governance agenda.

The contested ground is rhetoric, old writing on Egypt, and family. He tied a 2010 Oklahoma Sharia ban to white supremacy and wrote supportively of Egypt's 2011 revolution. His half-sister has posted that their mother was a Brotherhood activist in the early 1970s; he denies any family ties. His sharpest documented words are a 2018 retort to a rival who accused him of "civilization jihad," a March 2026 response to a synagogue attack that he later apologized for, and personal jabs at opponents; fact-checkers found no pattern of disrespecting women. See Rhetoric on race, gender and Israel.

**Pattern in his statements (added October 26, 2026).** Eight statements about his identity, credentials and results are checked against the record in section 22. Three conflict with the record or with his own other accounts, one is not supported as stated, and the rest are disputed. He often answers a different question when pressed. Nothing reviewed shows intent to deceive.

**Method.** Eight stages of his life, then the money, Sharia, the Brotherhood and each organization's legal status. Links are followed while they stay documented and involve public figures or organizations. Past the third step they stop being evidence about him, so the trace stops there.

**Evidence ratings used below**

- Documented: his own words on record, filings or primary documents.
- Reported: one outlet's finding not independently confirmed here; treat as a lead.
- Disputed: contested by fact-checkers or by other evidence.
- Not supported: found false, or no evidence located in this review.
