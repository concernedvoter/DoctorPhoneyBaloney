## Staff and first-hand accounts

**Bottom line.** Two recorded accounts from people near the campaign are on file. Neither is a statement by the candidate, and neither establishes wrongdoing. One adds nothing to the record on Medicare for All taxes. The other produced no evidence of foreign donors.

**Rules for this section.** A staffer's or volunteer's words are their own, not the candidate's, and are context only. Each entry states who the person is and how that was confirmed, how the recording was obtained, whether it is edited, what the campaign said, and what the account does and does not show. **What the clip adds on Medicare for All.** Little. Bridge Michigan's September 25, 2026 fact check rated Rogers' claim that the plan would raise taxes "likely correct," noted that he backed off a 2020 podcast line about ending private insurance, and said the campaign did not explain how it would pay for the plan. The closing line about actions in office is an opinion about the candidate, not evidence of intent to deceive.
