## Adjacent controversies

These sit outside the CAIR, Brotherhood and Sharia questions but recur in coverage of his record.

- **Hasan Piker.** He campaigned with the streamer on April 7, 2026, at Michigan State and the University of Michigan. That day he said he does not think 9/11 was justified and that appearing with someone does not mean agreeing with him on everything; at a campus event he declined to disavow Piker's views ([PolitiFact](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2026/aug/07/mike-rogers/abdul-el-sayed-9-11-hasan-piker-michigan/)). He has called Piker's remark that America deserved 9/11 dumb, but has not said whether he will campaign with him again ([Forward via Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/el-sayed-defends-opposing-israel-aid-as-he-woos-jewish-voters/article_ae02bfda-10ac-4228-906e-df28a1ca2284.html)). PolitiFact found that Rogers falsely tied him to Piker's 9/11 comments.
- **9/11 posts.** On September 11, 2021 he mourned the victims of attacks "perpetuated ignorantly in the name of my faith," and wrote that he would next mourn deaths caused in his country's name ([Salon](https://salon.com/2026/09/02/abdul-el-sayed-answered-9-11-question-honestly-cbs-attacked-him)). He told CBS both the attacks and the war on terror were awful. His campaign deleted all posts from before July 2023 ([Daily Wire](https://www.dailywire.com/news/abdul-el-sayed-has-a-deleted-9-11-tweets-problem)). Critics such as the [Washington Examiner](https://www.washingtonexaminer.com/?p=4710797) read the posts as false equivalence; defenders such as Salon read them as context.
- **2021 Arab American News op-ed.** Per the Free Beacon, he argued Arab and Muslim Americans bore the brunt of the post-9/11 era while radicalized white men were the neglected threat ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/in-deleted-social-media-posts-michigan-senate-candidate-abdul-el-sayed-repeatedly-drew-equivalence-between-9-11-and-the-us-response/)).
- **Israel****, Gaza and arms****.** He calls the war in Gaza a genocide; Jewish Insider reports he used the term before the war began ([Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2026/10/el-sayed-israel-genocide-before-gaza-war-began/)), and in a 2018 Detroit synagogue appearance he said he would condemn Hamas "in the same way" he condemns some human rights abuses by the Israeli military, which a Free Beacon headline presented as calling the two the same ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/?p=2167456)). A spokesperson said he opposes all U.S. arms sales to Israel, including Iron Dome interceptors, not only aid ([Jewish Currents](https://jewishcurrents.org/in-democratic-primaries-leading-candidates-oppose-selling-iron-dome-interceptors-to-israel)). He opposes unconditional military aid to Israel, Egypt, Saudi Arabia and Jordan, and says he could support aid to Israel after an agreement guaranteeing Israelis and Palestinians equal rights ([Forward via Detroit Jewish News](https://www.thejewishnews.com/news/el-sayed-defends-opposing-israel-aid-as-he-woos-jewish-voters/article_ae02bfda-10ac-4228-906e-df28a1ca2284.html)). AIPAC spent more than $30 million against him in the primary, the AP reported ([Watermark](https://watermarkoutnews.com/2026/08/05/progressives-score-victory-as-abdul-el-sayed-wins-michigan-senate-primary/)).
- **October 7.** In a 2025 statement on the attack's second anniversary he called it heinous and said he condemned it then and condemns it now; a campaign fundraising email sent that day did not mention Hamas or the attack, and a spokesperson said it went out by mistake ([Jewish Insider](https://jewishinsider.com/2025/10/michigan-senate-candidate-el-sayeds-oct-7-fundraising-email-ignores-hamas-attack-blames-israel/)).
- **Second Amendment.** In an April 2023 YouTube video, since removed, he floated amending the Constitution to replace the right to bear arms with a right to health care. In 2018 he backed an assault weapons ban, red flag laws and expanded background checks. No record reviewed shows him proposing confiscation. His campaign says he respects the Second Amendment, and pressed by Fox's Jesse Watters on August 24, 2026, he steered to health care and did not disavow the 2023 idea ([The Reload, Aug 13, 2026](https://thereload.com/michigans-democratic-senate-candidate-floated-replacing-the-second-amendment/); [The Reload, Aug 27, 2026](https://thereload.com/michigan-senate-democratic-nominee-declines-to-disavow-proposal-to-replace-gun-rights/)). Michigan's own constitution separately protects the right to keep and bear arms ([Mich. Const. art. 1, sec. 6](https://legislature.mi.gov/documents/mcl/pdf/mcl-Article-I-6.pdf)).
- **Iran audio.** The Free Beacon published campaign audio in which he discussed staying quiet on Ayatollah Khamenei's death because of Muslim voters' feelings. He questioned how the recording was obtained ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/tag/dearborn/)). Reported.
