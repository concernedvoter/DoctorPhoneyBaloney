## Stage 6: Governor run and the years between, 2017 to 2022

His 2018 campaign won 30 percent of the Democratic primary vote and put him on the national circuit of Muslim civic groups, including two CAIR national events.

- **Launch.** He entered the governor's race after leaving the department in 2017. At the spring 2017 state party convention he called himself "an immigrant from North Africa" ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/?p=2166901)).
- **CAIR national banquet, October 28, 2017.** Billed as a keynote speaker at CAIR's 23rd annual banquet in Arlington, Virginia, alongside ISNA president Azhar Azeez ([CAIR](https://www.cair.com/press_releases/michigan-gubernatorial-candidate-abdul-el-sayed-to-speak-at-cairs-23rd-annual-banquet/)).
- **Colbeck clash, 2018.** In April 2018 Republican rival Patrick Colbeck alleged Brotherhood ties; see the Brotherhood section. At an all-candidate forum in East Lansing on May 10, 2018, after Colbeck spoke of "civilization jihad," El-Sayed asked the Republican candidates to condemn Islamophobia and racism, said his oath to the Constitution guarantees his right to pray as he chooses, and told the field: "You may not hate Muslims, but Muslims hate you" ([Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2018/05/10/islamophobia-charge-rocks-michigan-governor-debate/34765749/)). Fox News, quoting the video, gives the line as "Muslims definitely hate you" ([Fox News](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/unearthed-clip-exposes-clash-between-el-sayed-ex-gop-rival-muslims-hate-you)), which matches a two-minute clip reviewed for this dossier. Republican Brian Calley said afterward he disagreed with Colbeck. Quote the full forum video, with Colbeck's preceding remarks, rather than either article.
- **Endorsements.** Bernie Sanders endorsed him in July 2018 ([Metro Times](https://www.metrotimes.com/news-hits/archives/2018/07/26/bernie-sanders-endorses-democrat-adbul-el-sayed-for-michigan-governor)), as did Emgage PAC, which calls itself the largest Muslim American PAC ([Emgage Action](https://emgageaction.org/?p=1284)).
- **Money.** He pledged to refuse corporate PAC money. An AP review of his state filings found none and showed $4.3 million raised as of July 22, 2018 ([AP via WXYZ](https://www.wxyz.com/news/michigan-democratic-candidates-spar-over-corporate-donations)). The Michigan Education Association PAC gave $10,000 ([Michigan Capitol Confidential](https://www.michigancapitolconfidential.com/even-after-right-to-work-unions-still-big-political-spenders-in-michigan-20190206)).
- **Result.** 342,179 votes, 30.2 percent, second to Gretchen Whitmer in the August 7, 2018 Democratic primary ([Michigan Department of State](https://mielections.us/election/results/2018PRI_CENR.html)).
- **CAIR conference, October 19 to 20, 2018.** Billed as a speaker at CAIR's National Leadership and Policy Conference and 24th annual banquet ([CAIR](https://www.cair.com/press_releases/update-michigan-gubernatorial-candidate-abdul-el-sayed-to-speak-at-cairs-national-leadership-and-policy-conference-annual-banquet-in-va-oct-19-20/)).
- **Southpaw Michigan.** He launched this PAC in September 2018 to support progressive candidates and causes ([CBS Detroit](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/el-sayed-creates-pac-to-boost-liberal-candidates-causes/)).
- **Media.** CNN commentator ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/michigan-senate-candidate-backed-by-bernie-sanders-to-speak-at-islamist-convention-alongside-hamas-sympathizers/)); launched Crooked Media's America Dissected podcast in 2019 ([Michigan Advance](https://michiganadvance.com/2019/09/23/el-sayed-to-launch-10-episode-crooked-media-podcast/)); named to the 2020 Biden-Sanders Unity Task Force on health care ([Biden campaign release, via the American Presidency Project](https://presidency.ucsb.edu/node/366863)).
- **2021 writing.** An op-ed in Dearborn's Arab American News on the post-9/11 era, a September 11 post, and an October Substack post calling Mohamed Soltan his friend. See the Brotherhood and Adjacent sections.
- **May 2021.** Spoke at a Dearborn rally protesting President Biden alongside Osama Siblani, per the [Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/abdul-el-sayed-quietly-headlines-fundraiser-alongside-hamas-loving-activist-who-urged-jews-to-be-sent-back-to-poland-speaker-who-backs-islamic-militants/).
- **CAIR-Oklahoma, October 22, 2022.** Keynote at the chapter's 16th annual awards banquet ([PolitiFact](https://politifact.com/factchecks/2026/aug/10/tweets/abdul-el-sayed-sharia-muslim-cair-michigan-senate/)). His candidate disclosure lists at least $5,000 for a CAIR-Oklahoma speech; whether that payment covered this event is unclear ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/michigan-senate-candidate-abdul-el-sayed-compared-an-oklahoma-ban-on-sharia-law-to-the-trail-of-tears/)). A timestamped transcript of the full 16-minute [speech video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e6qTkLr__CM) confirms the reported Trail of Tears line at 8:09. He placed the ban alongside the forces behind the Trail of Tears, the Tulsa massacre and the Oklahoma City bombing, blamed those forces rather than the voters, and said he did not know what the ban meant. He urged attendees to vote without saying for whom, and closed on the Constitution's preamble and Muslims' loyalty to the country. The speech does not mention the Brotherhood, Sharia as a legal system for the U.S., Israel or Egyptian politics.
- **CAIR's founding-era history: Holy Land Foundation case.** In November 2008, Holy Land Foundation for Relief and Development and five of its officials were convicted on all 108 counts in federal court, including material support for Hamas (the first 2007 trial had ended in a mistrial on most counts). Trial testimony by FBI Agent Lara Burns, walking through a 1993 FBI-surveilled Muslim Brotherhood meeting in Philadelphia, describes CAIR's founding as emerging from that same Palestine Committee network (her inference: the Philadelphia transcript itself never names CAIR, and CAIR was incorporated in 1994), and recorded CAIR co-founder Omar Ahmad telling the meeting the group would send "two messages, one to the Americans and one to the Muslims." The Justice Department named CAIR on a list of 246 unindicted co-conspirators and joint venturers filed with its 2007 trial brief; CAIR asked the court to strike its name, and in July 2009 Judge Solis sealed the list but declined to remove the names, finding a prima facie case of CAIR's involvement; the Fifth Circuit's 2010 ruling, in an appeal brought by another group, later called a similar "ample evidence" statement about that group outside the bounds of the question. Source: Center for Security Policy, C.A.I.R. Is Hamas (2016), reproducing the Burns trial testimony and Philadelphia meeting exhibits. Rating: Documented (the 2008 convictions, the unindicted-co-conspirator listing, the 2009 sealing order and the Fifth Circuit ruling); Disputed (the publisher's title claim that this "proved" CAIR itself is a terrorism front: CAIR was never indicted, tried or convicted). No connection to El-Sayed, who was not involved; he first spoke at CAIR events in 2017.
