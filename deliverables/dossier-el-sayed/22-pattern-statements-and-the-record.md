## Pattern: his statements and the record

**Bottom line.** The record supports a narrower finding than "truth is a weakness." In eight items he describes himself, his credentials or his results in terms the record does not fully support, and when pressed on several he answers a different question or does not answer. Three items (1 to 3) show a conflict with the record or between his own accounts, one (5) is not supported as stated, and the rest are disputed or unresolved. Nothing reviewed shows intent to deceive, and this dossier does not use the word "lied". Each row is rated on its own; the weakest link governs.

**How he answers when pressed.** Aides declined to answer on the 2012 Morsi letter (New York Times). At the Oct. 21 debate he did not respond to Rogers on the physician and dual citizenship charges. He steered to health care when asked about the 2023 video. In the material reviewed, the campaign has not said where he was born or why his name was changed, and did not respond to the Free Beacon on the 2017 speech. At Caro he answered "Sure, yeah, absolutely," then challenged the questioner. Rating: Documented as non-answers. A non-answer bears on candor, not on whether a statement was false.

**Direction and limits.** The overstatements run toward flattering his record or identity: credentials, results, the immigrant story. None conceals a crime or personal gain in the material reviewed. Rows 1, 2 and 4 rest on the Free Beacon alone for the framing and need a second source. Row 3 rests on Bridge Michigan. Parents' occupations (mother a nurse in Missouri, father came to build cars) are not counted because they do not clearly contradict the record. This test has not been run on Rogers here; run it before using this section in a comparison.

**Counterweights.** On the record he said "Sure, yeah, absolutely" to disavowing the Brotherhood and "I clearly disavow" the movement. His published research includes null and inconvenient findings (P08). His campaign called the citizenship statement an error. He walked back the Stevens remark.

**Questions for the campaign, in writing.** (1) Where was he born, and why was his name changed in 1991? (2) What is the basis for the $700 million wording after the Bridge finding? (3) When did he learn he was not a dual citizen, and from whom? (4) What did "immigrant from North Africa" mean in 2017?
