## Imams and clerics: contact and their records on Sharia

This section lists imams and clerics with documented contact with El-Sayed, what each is reported to have said, and whether any is on record calling for Sharia or Islamic rule to govern the United States. A shared program is not an endorsement.

**Finding.** None of the imams with the closest documented ties is reported to have called for imposing Sharia; their reported statements concern Hamas, Israel, Iran and homosexuality. Two clerics who shared convention programs with him, Siraj Wahhaj and Zaid Shakir, are on record favoring Islamic rule in America, in statements from 1992 and 2006.
