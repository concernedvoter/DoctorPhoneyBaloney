## Stage 7: Wayne County, 2023 to 2025

He ran Wayne County's Department of Health, Human and Veterans Services from March 2023 to April 2025, then was paid by the county as a consultant while campaigning.

- **Tenure.** Wayne County announced on December 1, 2022 that he had joined as a project consultant and would become director on March 1, 2023, pending County Commission approval ([Wayne County](https://www.waynecountymi.gov/News/Executive/2022/Dr.-Abdul-El-Sayed-joins-Health-Human-Veterans-Services-Department)). He served as director and as the county's health officer until April 3, 2025 ([CBS Detroit](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/wayne-county-health-director-abdul-el-sayed-resigns/)).
- **Consulting after exit.** The department paid him $9,000 a month, $72,000 in total, through his firm AME Higher LLC ([Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2026/07/28/abdul-el-sayed-personal-financial-disclosure-haley-stevens-michigan-democratic-senate-race/91076078007/); [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/politics/articles/abdul-el-sayed-paid-9k-180211076.html)); his 2026 Senate report lists $72,000 in county consulting fees. The county designee signed the contract on April 21, 2025 (he signed April 11), days after he filed his Senate candidacy on April 17; the purchase order is dated April 23 and was approved May 13. The contract provides for 30 hours a month of support on a leadership transition ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/abdul-el-sayed-billed-county-taxpayers-9000-a-month-in-consulting-fees-while-running-for-senate/)). A local watchdog newsletter, working from four invoices released under FOIA, says the County Commission does not appear to have approved it; its invoices run August to December 2025 and total $36,000, and it dates his resignation to August, which conflicts with other records ([Wayne County Watch](https://waynecountywatch.substack.com/p/el-sayeds-secret-contract-with-wayne)). No illegality has been established. He also reported $82,000 in consulting fees from Illinois-based One Health Management.
- **Medical debt.** His campaign cites a county program that canceled medical debt for thousands of residents as a signature achievement ([Hoodline](https://hoodline.com/2026/06/el-sayed-s-big-pac-backer-runs-firm-chasing-patients-medical-bills/)).
- **Speaking.** His Senate disclosure lists 2024 speeches to the American Medical Student Association ($5,000), the Ford Foundation ($5,000) and the Wisconsin Public Health Association ($4,500) ([Senate disclosure for 2025](https://michiganadvance.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/eFD-Annual-Report-for-2025-El-Sayed-Abdul.pdf)).
- **Juvenile detention.** A March 2023 county emergency order put him in charge of an incident command at the county's juvenile detention facility, with $10 million in emergency funding. That month a state investigator found the facility "flooded with sewage and garbage" ([Free Beacon](https://freebeacon.com/democrats/flooded-with-sewage-and-garbage-michigans-el-sayed-says-he-rebuilt-a-troubled-detroit-area-juvenile-jail-records-say-otherwise/)). In June 2023 he cited a 35 percent staff raise and about 40 new hires ([BridgeDetroit](https://www.bridgedetroit.com/wayne-county-health-leader-works-to-reform-juvenile-justice/)). After a 12-year-old was assaulted in September 2023, the state moved the facility to a provisional license that November and later found that top leaders knew of the dangerous conditions; the coverage does not name him ([CBS Detroit](https://www.cbsnews.com/detroit/news/wayne-county-juvenile-detention-center-could-lose-license-new-investigation-mdhhs/)). By February 2024 it was on a second provisional license ([Outlier Media](https://outliermedia.org/wayne-county-juvenile-detention-center-jail-conditions-license/)). He has claimed credit for rebuilding the facility's operations; the state records show continuing violations.

**Appointment vote.** The County Commission approved his appointment as director on February 16, 2023, at $200,000 a year plus paid parking and the executive benefit plan. It was one item in a bundle of eight resolutions that passed 13 to 0 with two commissioners excused, and the journal records no separate vote on his item (Wayne County Commission journal, File 2023-01-010). Rating: Documented.

**Health rankings.** Wayne County ranked last among Michigan counties in every County Health Rankings edition checked: 82 of 82 in 2015, then 83 of 83 in 2017, 2018 and 2022. The program has since stopped publishing numeric ranks. His arrival in 2023 and departure in 2025 did not change that standing, and a county ranking reflects far more than one department (County Health Rankings). Rating: Documented.

**Consulting contract terms.** The contract, purchase order and an invoice released under FOIA show a comparable-source award with a 30-hour monthly minimum and the county's required ethics clauses (Wayne County Watch documents). County procurement rules let the County Executive approve service contracts under $150,000 without a Commission vote, which may explain why none appears; that is analysis, not a finding (Wayne County Procurement Ordinance). Whether the process was proper has not been established. Rating: Documented as to terms.

**AME Higher LLC and Incision Media LLC.** The Michigan business registry shows AME Higher LLC formed September 6, 2018, active and in good standing with an annual statement filed every year from 2019 to 2026. Incision Media LLC was formed September 12, 2022, also active and in good standing, and the registry lists Incision Media as a name tied to AME Higher. Both use a commercial registered agent, so the public search shows no owner or member names (Michigan LARA). His Senate reports list both firms among his assets: the 2025 report shows AME Higher salary of $56,000 and a $6,000 draw; the 2026 report shows $64,000 in salary and a $103,000 member draw, plus $72,000 in Wayne County consulting fees (Senate disclosures). His campaign committee's FEC filings show no payments to either firm, only reimbursements to him totaling $90,572.94 over 25 payments (FEC). Whether the county fee is also counted in the firm's salary and draw cannot be determined from public records. Rating: Documented.

Rating: Documented.
