# Mike Rogers: Quick Dossier

As of 2026-10-22

## Snapshot

Mike Rogers is the Republican nominee for Michigan's open U.S. Senate seat, facing Democrat Abdul El-Sayed on November 3, 2026. He is a former Army officer, FBI agent and seven-term congressman who chaired the House Intelligence Committee. He runs on security, cost of living and immigration enforcement.

Bottom line: a long pro-life and pro-gun record, and a national security hawk. Since 2024 he has pledged not to change Michigan's abortion amendment, and since September 2026 he has called for ending the Iran war quickly.

## Record and controversies

The main questions about his record are his defense of CIA interrogation, the Brotherhood attacks on his opponent, and detention-industry donors.

- **CIA interrogation, December 2014.** As Intelligence chairman he called releasing the Senate's report "a terrible idea" and the program "legal, authorized, and appropriately briefed" ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/mike-rogers-releasing-senates-cia-torture-report-a-terrible-idea/)). He defended its legality, not the morality of the techniques. Documented.
- **NSA surveillance, 2013.** Wrote that the programs work "without infringing on Americans' privacy" and led opposition to the Amash amendment to end bulk phone records collection. His own recorded vote on it is unconfirmed. Reported.
- **Brotherhood attacks on El-Sayed.** In the Oct. 21 debate: "Are you for America, or are you for these terrorist groups overseas?" and afterward "This is not about the way you pray; it's the way you think." His one-step points are documented: the 2012 Morsi letter and the 2021 Soltan post. His claims that El-Sayed was "bought off" or has Hamas donors had no evidence shown. Partly supported.
- **Detention-industry donors.** $5,000 from the CoreCivic PAC, $10,000 from CoreCivic's CEO to his joint fundraising committee, and $5,625 from the GEO Group PAC, per FEC records reviewed by The Gander. Both companies run ICE detention. Reported.
- **Outside support.** SBA Pro-Life America is spending $4.5 million for him despite his abortion pledge ([Detroit News](https://www.detroitnews.com/story/news/politics/2025/09/24/anti-abortion-group-susan-b-anthony-michigan-senate-seat-mike-rogers-democratic-extremists/86316301007/)); AIPAC backing was reported in August 2026 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/22/mike-rogers-aipac-michigan-senate)). Reported.
- **Opponent's claim.** El-Sayed's Oct. 19 ad says Rogers "supported abortion bans with no exceptions, even to save the mother's life." His 2000 Michigan Catholic Conference questionnaire included a life-of-the-mother exception. Disputed.

## Catholic alignment

On life and family teaching he sits well ahead of his opponent; on social teaching he trails on several prudential issues. Full scorecard: deliverables/graphics/catholic-scorecard.md.

- **Aligned:** marriage and sexual difference; religious liberty; the right of legitimate defense (Catechism 2264 to 2265).
- **Partly aligned:** abortion (strong record, but a pledge not to change Michigan's amendment); immigration (enforcing the law fits Catechism 2241, while the bishops object to indiscriminate deportation); health care; Israel; labor; creation; truthfulness.
- **In tension:** IVF promotion; early support for the Iran war, which Pope Leo XIV called unjust; the 2014 defense of CIA interrogation.
- **No clear position found:** euthanasia, the death penalty, school choice.

The Church does not endorse candidates. Faithful Citizenship asks Catholics to weigh abortion as the pre-eminent priority and to consider character and integrity too.

## Open questions

- Would he back any federal limit on abortion consistent with Michigan's amendment, such as a ban on taxpayer funding?
- Where does he stand on euthanasia, the death penalty and school choice?
- Does he support reversing the 2025 Medicaid cuts that affect about 2.4 million Michiganders?
- His individual House votes on partial-birth abortion (2003), the marriage amendments, stem cell funding and the Amash amendment are unconfirmed on the clerk record.
